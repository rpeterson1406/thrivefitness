THRIVE Women Hiking Retreats

Connect • Explore • Restore

Created for women who say YES to thriving at any age. Do you want to live boldly, grow stronger, and invest in lifelong vitality? Then join us at the next THRIVE Women Retreats where we combine guided hikes, strength and mobility sessions, nourishing meals, and downtime to recharge.

We understand the changes in energy, sleep, and recovery that happen in our 40's, 50's, and 60's. Our small-group retreats provide the space and collective energy to help rejuvenate your mind, body, and spirit —so you return home feeling stronger, refreshed, and ready for what’s next.

We limit our hikes to 10 amazing women so you are supported 24/7.
